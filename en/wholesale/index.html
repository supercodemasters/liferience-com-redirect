Industries

IT and software for wholesalers and distributors

B2B customers order themselves, at their own prices.

Wholesalers take orders by phone, email and Excel, with different prices per customer. When customers instead order in their own portal at their contract prices, much manual entry disappears.

Common challenges

  • Orders arrive by email and phone
  • Customer-specific prices handled by hand
  • Stock is uncertain when ordering

What can be built

  • A B2B portal with customer prices and order history
  • Orders straight to warehouse and finance
  • Inventory and purchase planning
  • Integrations with customers’ and suppliers’ systems

Relevant experience

  • Supplier portal for 500+ suppliers (ITG, Turkey)
  • Purchasing and inventory in an ERP system (Samsara Solutions, Bulgaria)

Frequently asked questions

Can each customer have their own prices?

Yes. Contract prices, discounts and assortment can be set per customer.

Does it work with our ERP?

The portal connects to your ERP via API or files.
