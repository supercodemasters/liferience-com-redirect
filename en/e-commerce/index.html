Industries

IT and software for online retailers

A web shop that sells without creating extra work.

Online retailers often grow faster than their systems. Orders, stock, shipping, returns and bookkeeping run in separate tools, and every new order creates manual work.

Common challenges

  • Orders and returns handled by hand
  • Stock and web shop out of sync
  • Bookkeeping gets records late

What can be built

  • Sync of orders, stock and shipping
  • A returns flow with automatic refunds
  • Bookkeeping records per day
  • A dashboard for sales, margin and returns

Relevant experience

  • Health products marketplace with several sellers and delivery tracking (Alimila, Turkey)
  • Stock and price sync with Magento (PopLeads, Turkey)

Frequently asked questions

Can you build a whole web shop?

Yes, or connect the one you already have to the rest of your systems.

How are returns handled?

The customer registers in a form; stock, refund and bookkeeping update automatically.
