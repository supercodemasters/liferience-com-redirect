Industries

IT and software for shops

Store, warehouse and web showing the same thing.

Physical shops that also sell online need the same stock, prices and customers in both channels. Otherwise items that do not exist get sold, and campaigns must be entered twice.

Common challenges

  • Stock differs between till and web shop
  • Campaigns are entered in several places
  • Hard to see what sells

What can be built

  • Stock and price sync between till and web shop
  • Click and collect in store
  • Loyalty club and offers
  • A dashboard for sales per store and product

Relevant experience

  • Jewellery shop in Magento with one-step checkout and gift cards (59 Souls, USA)

Frequently asked questions

Do we have to change till system?

No, if the till has an API or can export data.

Can the customer see if the item is in store?

Yes. Store stock can be shown on the product page.
