Industries

IT and software for restaurants and cafés

Bookings, orders and purchasing with less waste.

Restaurants and cafés have thin margins. Table booking, takeaway orders, purchasing and waste affect the result directly, and much is still done by phone and paper.

Common challenges

  • Table booking by phone during the rush
  • Takeaway orders through many channels
  • Waste and purchasing not followed up

What can be built

  • Online table booking with seating rules
  • Your own takeaway ordering
  • Purchasing and waste per week
  • A dashboard for sales per day and hour

Frequently asked questions

Do we pay commission on our own orders?

No. Your own ordering on your website has no per-order commission, only the payment provider’s fee.

Can it connect to the till?

If the till has an API or can export data, yes.
