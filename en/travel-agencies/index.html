Industries

IT and software for travel agencies and tourism firms

Trips, bookings and documents gathered per customer.

Small travel agencies and tourism firms build trips from many parts: transport, accommodation, activities. Bookings, payments and travel documents need to fit together per customer and trip.

Common challenges

  • Parts of the trip booked in different systems
  • Travel documents sent by hand
  • Payments and instalments tracked by hand

What can be built

  • Booking of packages and activities
  • A customer portal with travel documents
  • Instalments and reminders
  • Integrations with suppliers’ systems where possible

Frequently asked questions

Can the customer see their trip in a portal?

Yes, with itinerary, documents and payment status.

Does it work for activity companies?

Yes, for example guided tours with places and times.
